SECTION III. APPLICATION AND SELECTION PROCEDURE
Article 5. Application Process
For Call 3, Applicants shall skip the Initial Onboarding step and submit their application directly via
the official online form. The required form Incubation Programme Registration Form - Call 3.
Article 6. Selection Mechanism
6.1 Applications will be assessed and ranked independently once the call has closed, provided that
they have submitted an Executive Summary (maximum 5 pages), Presentation Video (maximum 1min)
and a Company Deck (Optional).
6.2. For Call 3, a maximum of thirty-six (36) Applicants will be selected across all Incubation Sites,
distributed as follows:
• Barcelona: up to 17 Participants (3 places are reserved for winners of the previous
Acceleration Programme edition, as established in the respective rules; if none of the 3
winners exercise their right, the number may be extended to up to 20 places, following the
ranking order established by the evaluation panel)
• Guimarães: up to 12 Participants
• Lisbon: up to 5 Participants
• Bucharest: up to 2 Participants
Applications shall be evaluated and ranked independently for each Incubation Site.
6.3. Eligibility Verification
Upon closure of the application period, the Organizer shall verify that all submitted applications
comply with all mandatory eligibility criteria set out in Article 3 of these Rules.
Only applications that fully meet such criteria shall be considered eligible applications and proceed
to the selection stage.

[p6]

6.4. Direct Admission in Case of Insufficient Applications
In the event that the total number of eligible applications for a given Incubation Site is equal to or lower
than the number of available places allocated to that Site, all eligible Applicants for that Incubation
Site shall be directly admitted to the Incubation Programme, without the need for an external
evaluation process, provided that they meet the eligibility requirements.
6.4. Evaluation in Case of Competitive Selection
Where the number of eligible applications for a given Incubation Site exceeds the number of available
places allocated to that Site, the eligible applications for that Incubation Site shall be subject to a
competitive evaluation process carried out by a panel of BSC AI Factory staff members (hereinafter,
the “Evaluation Panel”).
The Evaluation Panel shall act in accordance with objective and impartial criteria, and its decisions
shall be final and binding.

6.6. Ranking and Selection
Following completion of the evaluation conducted by the Evaluation Panel for each Incubation Site, a
ranking of eligible applications for that Site shall be established, ordered from highest to lowest score.
The highest-ranked applications for each Incubation Site, up to the number of available places
allocated to that Site, shall be provisionally selected for participation in the Incubation Programme.
6.7. Waiting List and Replacement Procedure
In all cases where the number of eligible applications for a given Incubation Site exceeds the number
of available places allocated to that Site, an official ranked waiting list shall be established for that
Incubation Site based on the final evaluation scores obtained during the selection process.

[p8]

Applicants included in this Site-specific waiting list may be invited to join the Incubation Programme
in the following circumstances:
• Withdrawal or non-acceptance by a provisionally selected Applicant;
• Failure to complete onboarding requirements within the established timeframe;
• Voluntary withdrawal during the Batch;
• Temporary suspension or definitive exclusion of a Participant in accordance with Section V
and Annex 1.
Vacant positions shall be offered to the next highest-ranked Applicant on the waiting list
corresponding to the relevant Incubation Site, following the established ranking order, until all
available places for that Site have been filled.
The validity of each Site-specific waiting list shall extend throughout the entire duration of the Batch.
6.8 Site-specific Evaluation and Selection
Eligible applications shall be evaluated and ranked separately for each Incubation Site.
Selection decisions, waiting lists, and replacement procedures shall be managed independently at
Site level, according to the number of available places in each location.
Participation in one Incubation Site does not grant access to facilities, services, or activities
provided at other Incubation Sites.
Article 7. Resolution, acceptance & Appeal Procedure
7.1. The final resolution of the process will be posted on the official BSC AI Factory website and
individual notifications being sent by email to the selected Applicants.
7.2. Selected Applicants will be granted five working days from the date of notification to formally
accept or decline the offer. Failure to formally accept the offer within this 5-days period will be
construed as a definitive rejection.
7.3. Applicants who disagree with the outcome of the selection process may submit a formal request
for review (hereinafter, the “Appeal”) within five (5) working days from the date of notification of the
selection results.
Appeals must:
• Be submitted in writing via email to the official contact address indicated in the Call.
• Clearly state the grounds for disagreement.
• Include any supporting documentation deemed relevant.
Upon receipt of the Appeal, the Organizer shall review the application process exclusively in relation
to:
• Compliance with the eligibility criteria established in Article 3.
• Correct application of the evaluation procedure described in Article 6.

[p9]

The Organizer shall issue a reasoned written response within a maximum period of ten (10) working
days from the date of receipt of the Appeal.
The submission of an Appeal shall not suspend the execution of the Incubation Programme or the
onboarding of provisionally selected Participants.
