SECTION I. DEFINITIONS AND SCOPE
Article 1. Definitions
a. The Incubation Programme: BSC AI Factory Incubation Space Programme
b. The Organizer: The BSC AI Factory Consortium, led by Barcelona Supercomputing Center
(BSC-CNS), and including Fundação para a Ciência e a Tecnologia (FCT), The Scientific and
Technological Research Council of Turkey (TÜBİTAK) and Institutul Național de Cercetare-
Dezvoltare în Informatică (ICI Bucharest).
c. Applicant: Any startup, entrepreneur or stakeholder related to AI seeking admission to the
Incubation Programme.
d. Participant/Beneficiary: An Applicant formally selected and admitted to the Incubation
Programme.
e. Batch: A group of beneficiaries participating concurrently in the Programme.
f. Hot desk: The shared workspace area available for reservation by Participants.
g. Occupancy Rate: The percentage of effective use of the Incubation Space by a Participant
during the Incubation Programme, calculated based on verified presence data, including but
not limited to hot desk reservations, access control records, and/or other monitoring tools
designated by The Organizer.
h. Space Manager: Ultimate responsible for the day-to-day management of the offered
Incubation Spaces, including incidence resolution and providing support to Participants.

[p4]

i. Incubation Site: The physical incubation location assigned to a selected Participant within
the Incubation Programme. Each Incubation Site shall operate independently in terms of
activities, onboarding process, occupancy monitoring, and space management.
For Call 3, the following Incubation Sites are available:
• Barcelona (Spain) – up to 17 (or up to 20, subject to Article 6.1) Participants
• Guimarães (Portugal) – up to 12 Participants
• Lisbon (Portugal) – up to 5 Participants
• Bucharest (Romania) – up to 2 Participants
Each Incubation Site shall constitute an independent operational Batch under the same Call.
Article 2. Scope
These Rules govern all phases of Call 3 across all available Incubation Sites, including application,
eligibility verification, selection process, and the rights and obligations of the selected Participants for
the duration of the Batch (October 5th, 2026, to March 31st, 2027).
Each Incubation Site shall operate independently with respect to onboarding activities, incubation
services, space usage, and local programming.
