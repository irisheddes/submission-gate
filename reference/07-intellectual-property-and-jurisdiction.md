# SECTION VII. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND JURISDICITION

SECTION VII.  INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND JURISDICITION

Article 13. Intellectual Property
All Intellectual Property rights, including but not limited to patents, copyrights, and trade secrets,
generated or developed by the Participants during their time in the Programme shall remain the
exclusive and absolute property of the respective Participant company. The Organizer makes no claim
to such property.
Article 14. Applicable law and Jurisdiction
These Rules of Participation shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Spain.
Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with these Rules, including their
interpretation, application or validity, shall be submitted to the competent Courts and Tribunals
corresponding to the place where the relevant Incubation Site providing the services to the Participant
is located.
