# SECTION V. CODE OF CONDUCT AND TERMINATION

SECTION V. CODE OF CONDUCT AND TERMINATION
Article 10. Code of Conduct
All Participants must strictly adhere to the Code of Conduct (Annex 1), which mandates the following
principles:
- Strict prohibition on sharing sensitive or proprietary information of other companies without
explicit consent.
- Prohibition of verbal/physical violence, intimidation, harassment, or discriminatory
behaviour.
- No use or presence of alcohol or drugs during working hours or within the space.
- Encouragement of mutual support, knowledge sharing, and cooperation.
- Responsibility for maintaining clean, organized workspaces and shared areas.
- Use of facilities and equipment must be respectful and for their intended purpose, in full
compliance with the general building regulations and common policies established by the
building management.
- No tax domiciliation of the Participants in the Incubation Spaces.
Article 11. Termination and exclusion
The Organizer reserves the right to expel any Beneficiary that fails to comply with any of the regulations
listed in the Code of Conduct. Failure to comply may lead to warnings, loss of privileges, or definitive
exclusion from the Incubation Programme.
Persistent and unjustified underuse of the Incubation Space, despite prior written notice from the
Organizer, may be considered a Moderate Breach under Annex 1, where such behavior negatively
impacts the efficient allocation of shared resources.
In the event of termination, exclusion, or voluntary withdrawal of a Participant, the Organizer reserves
the right to replace the vacant position by selecting the next eligible company from the official waiting
list corresponding to the relevant Incubation Site.
