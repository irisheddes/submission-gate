# ANNEX 1: CODE OF CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINARY POLICY

ANNEX 1: CODE OF CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINARY POLICY

This Code of Conduct (hereinafter, the "Code") is an integral and binding part of the Regulatory
Framework and Rules of Participation (the "Rules"). Adherence to this Code is a mandatory condition
for entry, participation, and continued Beneficiary status in the BSC AI Factory Incubation Space
Programme. Any violation of this Code constitutes a material breach of the Rules and may result in
disciplinary action up to and including immediate exclusion.
Article 15. Principles of professionalism and non-discrimination
15.1. The Programme operates a Zero -Tolerance Policy against all forms of discrimination,
harassment, intimidation, bullying, or verbal or physical violence based on any protected
characteristic (including but not limited to race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability,
or nationality).
15.2. All Participants must strictly adhere to relevant national, European, and local anti -
discrimination and anti-harassment legislation.
15.3. All interactions between Participants, Organizer personnel, and third parties must be
conducted with the highest degree of professionalism, courtesy, and respect.

Article 16. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Protection
15.1.  Participants shall strictly observe and maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary
information, trade secrets, project details, business plans, and any other non -public data
(collectively, "Proprietary Information") belonging to the Organizer or other Participants.
15.2.  The unauthorized disclosure, use, or exploitation of any third -party Proprietary Information
encountered within the Incubation Space is strictly prohibited and constitutes a material
breach of this Code and the Rules.

Article 17. Facilities use and Responsibility
17.1.  All provided facilities, equipment, and resources must be used exclusively for the business
purposes intended by the Programme. They must be utilized in a safe and responsible manner,
respecting the shared nature of the spaces.
17.2.  Participants are obligated to maintain the cleanliness, organization, and professional
standards of their assigned workspaces, Hot Desks, and all shared common areas.
17.3.  Participants shall be held financially and legally liable for any direct damage caused to the
Organizer’s facilities, equipment, or property due to negligence, wilful misconduct, or misuse.

Article 18. Health, Safety and Substance Policy
18.1.  The consumption or presence of illegal drugs is strictly prohibited within the Incubation Space
premises. The consumption of alcoholic beverages is generally prohibited, except for limited,
pre-approved social events organized or sanctioned by the Organizer.
[p14]


18.2.  Participants are prohibited from entering the premises or engaging in Programme activities
while under the influence of any substance that may impair judgment, safe conduct, or
professional behaviour.
18.3.  Participants must strictly adhere to all applicable health, fire, and safety regulations, as well
as any specific safety protocols mandated by the Organizer or the building management.

Article 59. Communication, Public Disclosure, and Use of Identity
19.1. Participants shall not publish, disseminate, or otherwise make public, through any medium,
including but not limited to social media, websites, press releases, marketing materials,
public presentations, or events, any content that refers to, relates to, or is associated with
the BSC AI Factory, the Incubation Programme, its facilities, activities, or community,
without the prior written authorization of the Organizer.
19.2. This obligation applies to:
• The use of the BSC AI Factory name, logo, trademarks, or visual identity
• Any public reference to participation in the Incubation Programme
• Photographs, videos, or recordings taken within the Incubation Space
• Statements or materials that may imply endorsement, partnership, or institutional affiliation
19.3. Where authorization is granted, Participants shall ensure that all published content is
accurate, non-misleading, and consistent with the information approved by the Organizer
and with applicable communication and visibility requirements.
19.4. Any publication or dissemination of content in breach of this Article, including failure to obtain
prior authorization or refusal to comply with a request for correction or removal, shall be
considered a Moderate Breach (Tier 2) under the Disciplinary Policy set forth in this Annex,
without prejudice to the Organizer’s right to require the immediate withdrawal, correction, or
amendment of the affected content.

Article 20. Disciplinary actions and Sanctions
20.1.  The authority responsible for reviewing alleged breaches, conducting investigations, and
determining the appropriate sanction is the BSC AI Factory Commission, advised by the Space
Manager. The BSC AI Factory Commission’s decision regarding the severity of the breach and
the corresponding sanction shall be final and binding.

20.3.  The Procedure for the application of the sanctions will be the following:
a. Upon notification of a potential breach, the Space Manager shall conduct an initial review. If
the breach warrants further action, the case will be presented to the BSC AI Factory
Commission.
b. The accused Participant shall be notified in writing of the allegations and shall be afforded a
minimum of three (3) business days to provide a written defense and/or clarification to the
BSC AI Factory Commission before a decision is rendered.
c. The BSC AI Factory Commission will evaluate the evidence, the Participant's defense, and the
context (including any prior warnings on record) to assign the appropriate Tier classification
and sanction.
d. Disciplinary action taken by the BSC AI Factory Commission is without prejudice to any civil or
criminal legal remedies available to the Organizer or any affected third party.
