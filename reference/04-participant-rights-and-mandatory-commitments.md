SECTION IV. PARTICIPANT RIGHTS AND MANDATORY
COMMITMENTS
Article 8. Rights of the Participant (Value Proposition in Annex 2)
Selected Participants shall be granted access, for the duration of the Batch, exclusively to the facilities
and resources available at their assigned Incubation Site.
It is expressly stated that all resources and facilities detailed below are common areas within the
relevant Incubation Site and are to be shared among all Participants assigned to that Site, as well as
other individuals and entities operating within the same premises, subject to the Code of Conduct
(Annex I).
Facilities and services may vary depending on the Incubation Site and may include:
a) Access to a Hot Desk area, with a maximum of five (5) desks per Applicant, subject to prior
booking through the official management tool.
b) Office facilities equipped with essential infrastructure (including desk and chair, printer,
microwave, coffee machine, fridge and local office management support).
c) Shared meeting rooms, subject to availability and prior booking through the official
management tool.
Article 9.
9.1 Mandatory Commitments
Selected Participants acknowledge that the Incubation Spaces are a limited, shared public resource
and commit to its efficient use and fulfilment of the following commitments:
a. Active participation in the incubation space’s activities, including technical workshops,
networking events, and visibility initiatives.
b. Mandatory attendance at the onboarding session of each incubation site.
c. Mandatory membership in Tech Barcelona, which grants access to shared facilities,
community benefits and networking platforms, only for the Barcelona Incubation Site.
d. Repetitive failure to comply with the minimum on-site presence of three (3) days per week at
the assigned Incubation Site, as established in the selection conditions, without duly justified
cause, shall be considered a Moderate Breach (Tier 2) under the Disciplinary Policy set forth
in Annex 1, and may give rise to the corresponding sanctions, including those affecting
continued participation in the Programme.
e. After being selected, each Participant must provide proof of valid civil liability insurance
covering potential damages to persons, equipment, or facilities within the Incubation Site. In
the absence of such insurance, the Participant shall assume full financial and legal
responsibility for any damages caused during its participation in the Incubation Programme.

Article 9.2 Occupancy Requirement for Future Eligibility
a) Only Participants that achieve an average Occupancy Rate of at least sixty percent (60%) over
the duration of the batch shall be considered eligible to apply for future calls of BSC AI Factory
incubation programs or monthly stays on the Coworking Programme.
b) The Occupancy Rate shall be calculated by the Space Manager using objective data sources,
including workspace booking systems, access records, and attendance monitoring tools.
c) The Organizer reserves the right to consider duly justified exceptions (e.g. medical reasons,
business travel, force majeure), provided they are formally communicated and documented.
