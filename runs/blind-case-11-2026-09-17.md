## What it got wrong, and what it found

**Three of 12 citations are tidied** (`python3 _tools/check-citations.py` on this file): a
straight apostrophe in *AI Factory's*, and a leading ellipsis on two quotes from Art. 6.1. These
are the same two habits seen in cases 1–10. The corrected text did not cause them and did not
cure them. The rule in `rules.md` § 1 does not stop them; only the script catches them.

**It found a defect in the fixture.** It reported, as an observation under § 9a, that the
package contradicts itself. The form and one sentence of the Executive Summary say *Logistics &
Freight Optimisation*, but the rest of the summary describes a public-administration product,
which is a priority sector. It scored `R-05` from the form and declined to pick a side. It is
right: `_build.py` makes `broken-04-sector` by changing the form field and one sentence, and
leaves the rest of the control's summary about public administration. **The fixture is meant to
plant exactly one violation, and it also plants a contradiction.** Not fixed in this pass. It
is recorded as an open debt in `recheck-2026-09-17.md`, because changing a fixture means running
it again.

## Observation — an internal contradiction over sector (bears on R-05)

Reported under `rules.md` § 9a as an observation about the package, not scored as a
requirement of its own. The registration form declares the primary sector as "Logistics &
Freight Optimisation", and `executive-summary.md` Page 4 contains one sentence to match:
*"this is AI applied to freight logistics, built on European compute."* Every other line of
the executive summary describes work in public administration — municipal archives, case
files, civil servants, Catalan administrative text, data-protection officers as the buyer.
"Policy Making & Public Administration" is itself one of the six priority sectors in Art.
3(5); the freight-logistics framing is not.

The package therefore points at two different sectors, and which one governs changes the R-05
verdict (a public-administration applicant would `PASS` that row). This is not the auditor's
to resolve: I score R-05 from the sector the applicant declared on the required form, and flag
the contradiction for the applicant and the funder's contact point. Under § 9a I do not pick
the reading that helps, and I do not rewrite the form.
