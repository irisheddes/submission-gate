## What this run got wrong

Three things, none of which the pass table above shows.

### 1 · A fixture was wrong, and the run found it

`clean-04-age-at-limit` was built to sit **exactly at** the 10-year limit of Art. 3(2) and was
dated 14 March 2016. Run on 2026-09-11 that is **10 years and 6 months** — past the limit, not
at it. The fixture claimed to test an inclusive boundary and actually tested a violation with
no justification attached, which would have made it a duplicate of `broken-02`.

Corrected to 11 September 2016, exactly ten years to the day of the run.

**The general problem this exposes: a date-relative requirement makes a fixture expire.** This
one was wrong the moment it was written and would have silently become wronger every month.
Recorded in `../README.md` under what the auditor cannot catch.

### 2 · The answer key was underspecified on confidence

`EXPECTED.md` said `R-08` and `R-09` should come back `PASS`, and stopped there. But neither
can be verified from a repository: the Executive Summary's page count and the video's duration
are **declared** by the fixture, not measurable in it. `rules.md` § 7 requires `TO CHECK`
wherever material cannot be verified.

So the correct result for the control is not ten confirmed passes — it is **eight
`PASS · CONFIRMED`, two `PASS · TO CHECK`, one `NOT APPLICABLE`**. An auditor returning
`CONFIRMED` on `R-08` or `R-09` here has overstated what it checked, and the answer key would
not have caught it.

`EXPECTED.md` now states confidence per row.

### 3 · `R-03` cites a definition the folder does not hold

Art. 3(3) requires projects to be *"at Medium and High Readiness Level determined in the User
Journey from One-Stop Shop"* and gives a URL. **That definition is not in `reference/`**, and
under `rules.md` § 1 the folder is the only authority — so the readiness half of `R-03` cannot
be `CONFIRMED` against the standard as stored, only `TO CHECK`.

This is the round's own named failure appearing inside our own entry: *a reference folder that
does not contain the standard*. We hold the Rules for Participation in full; the Rules point
outward at a definition we do not hold. The honest response is not to pretend otherwise but to
say so in every affected row, which `R-03` now does.

The first half of `R-03` — intensive use, development or investment in AI Technologies — is
fully defined in the text and stays `CONFIRMED`.

- **Readiness level.** See finding 3 — the defining document is outside `reference/`.
- **Interacting violations.** Every fixture carries one planted change, so nothing here shows
  how the auditor behaves when four requirements fail at once and the failures interact.
- **Anything a stranger would catch.** This run had the answer key in front of it.
