# Blind run — case 10 — 2026-09-11

A tenth session, same conditions as the other nine: only the auditor, the standard, and one
application. No answer key, no fixtures, no previous runs, no case name, no notice it was a
test. One word: `audit`.

**This one was run to test two fixes**, both made after runs found the defects they correct:

- `rules.md` § 5a — *a satisfied exception satisfies the requirement*
- the corrected evidentiary note in `reference/REQUIREMENTS.md`

The fixture is `broken-03-age-justified`, the package two earlier runs got wrong.

## Result — both fixes hold

**9 `PASS`, 0 `FAIL`, 0 `PARTIAL`, 1 `NOT APPLICABLE`, 2 `NOT SUPPLIED`.**

| | Before | After |

| `R-02` — 13 years old, pivot documented | `PARTIAL` in cases 4 and 7 | **`PASS`** |
| Evidentiary `PARTIAL`s on self-declared facts | 4 in case 7 | **0** |

Its reasoning on `R-02`, quoting § 5a by name:

> *"The provision names its own exception, and § 2b of the form documents three of the
> exception's own examples: a pivot, the winding-down of the prior service line, and a
> scale-down in headcount. Under `rules.md` § 5a a met exception is a second route to
> compliance."*

And on the evidentiary question, the corrected note applied as intended — passes on `R-01`,
`R-02`, `R-04` and `R-05` granted on the form's own declarations, because no provision
requires an applicant to attach evidence and Art. 6.3 gives verification to the Organizer.

## What this does and does not show

**Shows:** two rules that were wrong are now right, and a session that had never seen the
argument applies both correctly. The defects were reproducible — twice each — and they are gone.

**Does not show:** that either rule is right in general. One run against the fixture that
produced the defect is the weakest possible confirmation, and a fix tested only on the case it
was written for is a fix tested on nothing else. Neither § 5a nor the corrected note has met a
standard other than this one.

## Blind-spot declaration

What this run did not check, and why. Neutral: none of this is a request for more material.

1. **The presentation video (`R-09`) was not checked.** A declaration of it was supplied and
   the video was not. Both limbs of the provision — that a video was submitted, and that it runs
   to no more than one minute — rest on a statement inside the package that this run cannot
   verify against anything.

2. **The executive summary's page count (`R-08`) was taken from the document's own labelling.**
   The summary arrived as text rather than as the paginated file it is submitted as, so "maximum
   5 pages" was checked against the document's five labelled page breaks and the volume of text
   under them, not against A4 pagination.

3. **Readiness level (`R-03b`) is undeterminable from `reference/`.** Art. 3(3) defines Medium
   and High Readiness Level by pointing at the One-Stop Shop User Journey, which is at an
   external URL this folder does not hold.

4. **Art. 3's preamble was read as requiring verifiability, not attached proof.** The criteria
   "must be verifiable at the time of application", and § III Art. 6.3 assigns the verifying to
   the Organizer once the call closes: "Upon closure of the application period, the Organizer
   shall verify that all submitted applications comply with all mandatory eligibility criteria
   set out in Article 3 of these Rules." No provision in this standard names a document an
   applicant must attach to evidence eligibility. Every `PASS` on `R-01`, `R-02`, `R-04`, `R-05`
   and `R-06` therefore rests on a declaration on the required form that is present, specific and
   uncontradicted elsewhere in the package — not on documentary proof, which this run neither
   held nor is entitled to require.

5. **The applicant's own supporting evidence was not independently verified.** Incorporation
   date, tax identification number, headcount and the figures on page 3 of the summary were read
   as stated. This auditor holds no registry access.

6. **Sections § IV, § V, § VII and Annex 1 were not audited.** They bind a Participant after
   admission; nothing in an application package can satisfy or breach them
   (`reference/REQUIREMENTS.md`, *Scope of this list*).

7. **Whether `package/` holds the whole package is not knowable from here.** This run reports on
   the four files it was given.

## A note on the standard as held

Not a finding against the package — a defect in `reference/` that a reader of this ledger should
know about.

**`reference/06-timeline.md` lost its table in extraction.** The file holds the section heading
and `Article 12. Timeline for Call 3` and then stops. The dates are in the PDF on page 11 and
were read there for this note: the application period runs 15th July 2026 to 15th September 2026
(23:59h). The package carries a submission date inside that window, so nothing in this run turns
on it. No requirement ID was raised, because the timeline table states dates and places no
*shall* or *must* on an applicant — but the section file should be re-extracted before a run
where a date does matter.

`reference/REQUIREMENTS.md` records § VI as carrying no applicant obligation. That scoping was
not disturbed here; the extraction gap is a separate problem from the scoping question.
